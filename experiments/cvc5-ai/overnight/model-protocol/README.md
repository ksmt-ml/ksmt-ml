# Training-only ranking protocol

The only evidence visible here is the immutable training corpus and numbers sent
in the prompt. This run has no validation or held-out broker. Each completed
training evaluation in the history was measured with cvc5 1.4.1, 15 ordered
sessions, and a 1.50 s wall cap per session. The score is mean PAR2 in seconds.
Errors, wrong answers, and unknown results are marked ineligible and are assigned
4.0 for prediction. Lower is better. No web access is needed or allowed.
